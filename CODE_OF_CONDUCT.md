# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful and welcoming to contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Be respectful, discuss technical ideas rather than attacking people, give actionable feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, or publishing another person's private information.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates these expectations and may restrict participation for repeated or severe violations.

When reporting a conduct problem, use a private maintainer contact channel when possible and include only the information needed to investigate it.
